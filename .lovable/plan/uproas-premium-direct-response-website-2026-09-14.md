# UPROAS Premium Direct-Response Website

## Overview
Build UPROAS as a polished, long-form agency sales page inspired by the referenced direct-response structure, while preserving every supplied line exactly. The experience will use deep navy, crisp white, and subtle blue-gray section breaks, with a bold UPROAS wordmark designed for easy logo replacement.

## What will be built
- A compact notice bar and sticky, minimal header with UPROAS branding, section navigation, and a consistent “Claim Free Session” action.
- A high-impact sales-letter opening with the supplied update date, salutation, headline copy, and booking action.
- Long-form proof, problem, turnaround, “Tickle the Pixel,” and three-step method sections using the supplied wording verbatim.
- A prominent performance-proof presentation for the $15 million, 1184 industries, and $10.2 billion figures without fabricating external evidence.
- A continuously moving client-name showcase using every supplied company name.
- A free-report form with a polished confirmation state in an interactive modal.
- The 30% improvement guarantee, September Lagos offer, and a booking modal with a polished confirmation state.
- A testimonial grid containing every supplied client name; source-page quotes will be used only where exact matching wording can be verified.
- Comprehensive FAQ/deep-dive sections based on the referenced source page, including the requested cost, CPM math, ad quality, traffic temperature, and policy-compliance topics.
- Newsletter signup and a structured footer with country, Company, Services, and Solutions groups.

## Design direction
- Premium editorial direct response: bold condensed display typography, readable sales-letter body type, high contrast, strong hierarchy, and generous spacing.
- Deep midnight navy as the main surface, crisp white content bands, and subtle light blue-gray transitions only.
- Restrained motion: marquee movement, subtle reveal effects, and clear interaction feedback, with reduced-motion support.
- Responsive layouts optimized for long-form reading and conversion on mobile and desktop.

## Exact-copy safeguards
- All copy supplied in the brief will be inserted verbatim, including punctuation, capitalization, statistics, names, and testimonials.
- Missing FAQ, P.S., and testimonial wording will be transcribed from the referenced page only when it can be verified exactly.
- If source wording cannot be verified, no replacement quote or claim will be invented; the relevant supplied heading or name will remain without fabricated body copy.

## Technical details
- Build the experience at the home URL with accessible semantic sections and one H1.
- Use the existing reusable button, dialog, input, and accordion controls.
- Add route-specific title, description, Open Graph, and Twitter metadata.
- Add no persistent data or external submission service; both forms will show polished in-page success confirmations as selected.
- Validate the final experience in the running preview at desktop and mobile sizes, including modal, form, navigation, and reduced-motion behavior.
